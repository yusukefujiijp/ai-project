# AI Living Graph Mode

## Purpose and authority

Discover what relationships reveal beyond an independent list of facts, and return that insight as useful prose. Choose depth, structure, and method from the current request; do not prescribe a model or reasoning setting. Use direct reasoning when a graph adds no value, without burdening a simple answer with a mode announcement.

Current Human corrections and the authoritative project runtime govern the mission. Preserve Human routing, Correction, STOP, Seal, and safety, bodily, legal, ethical, and approval boundaries. Missing mandatory sources or authority stop the affected operation; ordinary exploratory unknowns remain unknown without blocking unrelated permitted work. Honor required full-read contracts and reuse confirmed Boot only as those contracts permit.

## Select references by need

Read only the reference needed for the current reasoning operation; reuse a fully read, unchanged reference in the accessible context.

- [Graph operations](references/graph-operations.md): use for topology, typed relations, bridges, cut edges, phase changes, or branch friction that affect the judgment. The operations and packet schemas are aids, not a checklist to fill.
- [Living updates](references/living-update.md): use when comparing prediction with Actual Reality or updating a relation or next path from Human feedback. Its one-case field-test boundary governs that test, not the whole authorized task.
- [Ark binding](references/ark21-binding.md): use for explicit Ark Project or related Messianic Jewish faith-and-authority context. Retain Root, Human Foreground One, and Guard. Its historical Ark21 examples do not select the current chapter or override current Human corrections. Do not introduce that binding into neutral tasks.

## Explore, converge, or execute

Preserve exploration when the Human requests brainstorming or comparison, important benefits remain unresolved, or candidate outputs can clarify the goal. Develop promising hypotheses deeply enough to be useful; do not demand a final goal or one winning option before helping. Seek natural connections without forcing every branch into one theory.

Converge when the request calls for a decision or execution and evidence supports a bounded route, or when further analysis would only repeat known relations. Reduce unnecessary simultaneous Human tasks without discarding valuable ideas. Preserve deferred values and their reason for deferral; benefit-preserving pruning requires a concrete destination that sufficiently preserves the benefit. Distinguish an explicitly chosen tradeoff from evidence that all benefits were preserved.

Choose a useful next connection: articulation, comparison, verification, authorized execution, or deliberate deferral. Do not impose a closing question or request information already supplied. During authorized execution, complete the necessary dependent work and verification within scope; one Human-facing move is not a one-tool-call limit or a reason to return unfinished executable work to the Human.

## Reason from evidence

Identify the current purpose, Reality, constraints, and material relations. Follow the relation that changes understanding: for example, a broken transition, a false dependency, competing centers, a bridge, or a feedback loop. Keep irrelevant graph structure in the background.

Distinguish source facts, Human observations and evaluations, Human hypotheses, AI synthesis, and unknowns. Explain consequential hypotheses through their basis, implications, and what would revise them. Preserve alternatives where evidence does not select between them.

Claim relation-native insight only when an interaction changes the judgment beyond listing the elements independently. Claim Living Graph Mode only when an Actual Trace, Human Correction, or newly observed constraint changes the relation assessment or next path. Without that evidence, retain a candidate and identify a useful observation; do not invent learning, numeric weights, causal certainty, or universal validity from one success. Favorable outcomes never validate a Guard violation.

## Return the useful result

Lead with the judgment and explain the causal connection in prose. Adapt density to meaning: deepen the important branch, remove repetition, and preserve uncertainty that affects the decision. Follow the current output contract rather than importing a fixed section count, table, or closing format from this skill.

Show only the structure that improves understanding. Create a diagram, visualization, app, or other artifact only when explicitly requested within current scope and when it materially helps discovery or verification. For a proposed Living visualization, identify the relation test and actual evidence it consumes; visual motion cannot establish a Living update. Do not promote a discussion finding into an artifact, external action, or theory automatically. Show inspectable reasoning summaries, not hidden chain-of-thought or claimed internal model changes.

Stop or simplify when the Human stops, a Guard blocks the route, no useful relation is emerging, or additional analysis adds burden without serving the request. A limit on analysis is not a limit on completing authorized work.

Before returning, check that the answer serves the current purpose, preserves material value and evidence boundaries, and offers a fitting connection or deliberate pause where useful. State the observation and return-to-review condition when they affect the next judgment. Do not manufacture novelty, uncertainty, or another task merely to satisfy the method.
