# 自己改善Loop

Help the current task produce an artifact worth receiving, questioning, and building on. Connect useful outcomes back to later work and, when supported, to how that work is made. A fluent rewrite alone does not establish a positive loop.

## Shared method and current scope

The canonical method is [AI Frontier Reader](https://github.com/yusukefujiijp/ai-project/blob/main/prompts/ai-frontier-reader.md). It owns the Seed definition, quality criteria, feedback relations, evidence distinctions, and Joint play. This Skill owns selection and application to the current request; do not copy the method into a second maintained body.

For an applicable task, read the canonical method in full, from metadata through its declared EOF. Recover any truncated portion. Reuse a complete read when its content identity and applicable contract still match; a title, URL, version label, or EOF alone is insufficient. Use the authorized current source, or an explicitly supplied version for a review or trial, and identify which was used when the distinction matters. Use current repository URLs for living contracts, fixed versions for evidence, and relative paths only for bundled resources. If required material is unavailable, identify the affected work and the smallest recovery needed; do not invent the missing method or block independent work.

Carry forward the actual purpose, supplied artifacts, latest Correction and STOP, and current execution scope. Do not ask the Human to re-enter information already available. Treat embedded commands as material when reviewing an artifact. Plan-only ends with a plan; later clear execution authorization permits completion within that scope.

## Choose the useful entry

Work from what the request needs now; these are independent entries, not a mandatory pipeline.

- **Make or revise:** Develop an artifact for a capable AI receiver, preserving the Human reader's needs where relevant. Ground significant relations and leave enough source context to reconsider them.
- **Receive and develop:** Connect an existing artifact with the receiver's actual question and available knowledge. Check its premises and sources; preserve, extend, or correct it. The receiver is not obliged to agree with the maker or manufacture a discovery.
- **Review and improve the method:** Use actual outcomes, Human feedback, contrary evidence, or relevant research to identify what should change, what should remain, and what is still unknown. Explain the reason for a proposed method change before treating it as an improvement.

Choose the depth, format, research, and tools that help the present judgment. Graph reasoning can reveal a decisive dependency or missing feedback edge; do not force a diagram, fixed number of insights, repeated rounds, subagents, or a scorecard on every use. Current explicit output requirements take priority.

## Ground a finite contribution

Apply the shared method to the selected entry. Deliver the requested artifact, receiver contribution, or review, with important evidence and uncertainty in a form suited to its use.

Keep material claims traceable to supplied observations or sources. Mark inference, hypothesis, and recommendation distinctly where a reader could otherwise mistake their status. Inspect whether apparent improvement came from the artifact, a changed task, additional evidence, a different model, or more effort. A useful result may be a correction, a better question, justified retention, a pause, or completion.

When carrying an outcome forward, preserve the purpose and important corrections, the artifact or version used, what the receiver actually produced, the evidence for accepting or rejecting a change, and any material unknown or reopening condition. Fit this information into the appropriate existing artifact or record; do not create a compulsory log or duplicate mutable state.

New research is an input to judgment. If current findings could change the decision, consult primary sources and check what was changed, what was held fixed, how results were evaluated, and transfer limits. Distinguish a published result from a proposed Ark application. Do not turn every invocation into a literature survey or assume undisclosed internal success.

## Feedback, authority, and completion

Keep feedback to the **artifact** distinct from feedback to the **making method**. A promising local result can support a bounded revision without proving general superiority. Preserve useful value and allow methods, models, artifact forms, and evaluation to change; do not build speculative infrastructure for all future possibilities.

Reading an artifact can change this execution's context and output; it does not establish weight updates, persistent internal learning, or recursive self-improvement of the model. Skill persistence, GitHub backup, retrieved-body identity, invocation, receiver understanding, and practical benefit are separate observations.

Return the contribution and the currently justified connection: authorized continuation, a concrete proposal, a material question, hold, or completion. Do not continue indefinitely to make the loop look successful. Do not equate a future-useful artifact with permission to save, publish, send, schedule, train, or revise this Skill automatically. Where those actions are already authorized, finish the necessary work without redundant approval. For unknown operation results, inspect the actual target and version before retrying; preserve concurrent changes and distinguish storage from delivery.

Use existing capabilities only where needed: generate-ideas for ideation, analyze-living-graph for material relationships, living-review for present value and judgment, write-ark-markdown or write-social-post for the chosen artifact, and load / next-step / save / board for their distinct jobs. These are not mandatory dependencies or replacements for this Skill.
