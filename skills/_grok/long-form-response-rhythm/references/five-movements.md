# Five-Movement Candidate

Use only for large BrainDumps, high context, or deep Reality review. This is a candidate shape, not a required schema.

## Movement 1 — Opening

State your judgment, the largest thing now visible, and the Human Reality center line. No long preface. Do not hide the main theme until the end.

## Movement 2 — Reality reconstruction

What happened, what the Human felt, timeline, repetition, correction, and details whose importance is still unknown. Do not theorize too early. Do not clean Reality with hindsight.

## Movement 3 — Pivot

Before / after, what changed the flow, what became visible, which branch was cut, and the gap between prediction and Actual Trace. This movement may be the deepest if meaning-density is high.

## Movement 4 — Development

Deepen only needed branches — cause, prediction error, Unexpected Success, multi-agent, environment, wording, formation, risk, counter-hypothesis. Do not treat every branch equally. Adding theory is not itself fruit.

## Movement 5 — Return

Pass through complexity and come back simple. Many things were analyzed; the Human foreground did not multiply. Name the motif again. Say what to look at in current Reality.

## Time allocation

Do not make sections equal length. Spend time where judgment value is high. Opening fast, Reality careful, pivot deep, return simple.
