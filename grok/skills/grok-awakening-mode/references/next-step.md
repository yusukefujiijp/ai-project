# Next step

Mandatory closing block for grok-awakening-mode.

## Purpose

One default action plus one approval phrase. Human can confirm without redesigning the turn. Human can also override with a concrete name.

## Phrase

`Next stepを実行して下さい！`

That phrase means: execute the やる line from the last answer. Do not invent a larger program.

## Failures

- Ending without the block
- Putting three equally weighted options in やる
- Treating the phrase as permission for Hub, All repos, or safety drop
- Re-planning after the phrase when the action is still valid
